Privacy Policy

This platform is dedicated to providing public access to public domain works with a commitment to radical transparency and user privacy. Our philosophy is simple: Offer the people free information, without collecting your data.

1. No Accounts, No Cookies

We do not use accounts, profiles, or registration systems. Your identity remains entirely anonymous to us.

  • No Personal Information: We never ask for your name, email, or any other identifying information.
  • No Cookies: We do not place cookies or tracking pixels on your device. Your browsing history on this site remains private.

2. "Revs" Statistics

To maintain our community Top 10 rankings (calculated by Day, Week, Month, and Year), we record "revs."

  • How it works: When a magnetURI is accessed, we increment a "rev" counter for that specific work.
  • Historical Data: Revs are recorded regardless of the work's original upload date.
  • Anonymity: These counts are purely numerical and aggregate. We do not link revs to IP addresses or specific user identities.

3. MagnetURIs and Infrastructure

This site does not link to or host public domain "files." Instead, we provide links to public domain magnetURIs. Once you leave this site to join a peer-to-peer swarm, your IP address is visible to other peers. This is a fundamental technical requirement of the BitTorrent protocol.

Server Logs: We do not maintain custom server logs. Our infrastructure runs on Amazon EC2, which maintains standard infrastructure logs required for basic network security. We do not analyze these logs for tracking or marketing purposes.

4. Future Roadmap

We are constantly evolving. Please note that once we integrate WebTorrent and MetaMask, this privacy policy will be updated to reflect how those technologies interact with your browser and the blockchain. Check back for updates as these features are rolled out.
